## A story from the shop. A page of your own.

This final team-edited film introduces the cultural side of HERE through a visit to a Hong Kong dai pai dong. A small “Tap for a story” marker invites a visitor to discover the story behind an everyday place. Reading, conversation and a meal turn an ordinary stop into a more personal encounter with the city.

The proposed experience connects NFC story markers with place-based cultural content and a visitor's own record of the visit. Together with HERE's indoor wayfinding prototype, it explores how finding a place can lead to understanding and remembering it.

## What this video demonstrates

The film presents the intended visitor experience and cultural storytelling concept. It is separate from the repository's working indoor localization and navigation demonstration; it does not establish that an NFC deployment, cultural-content backend or combined end-to-end service has been implemented or field-tested.
