用后置主摄，保持 1×Use the rear main camera at 1×
普通照片模式。尽量沿用建图时的镜头，不用人像、美颜、超广角或数字变焦。Use normal photo mode and the same lens as the mapping video where possible. Avoid portrait mode, beauty filters, ultra-wide lenses and digital zoom.
先拍1张清晰的环境照Start with one clear scene photo
手机保持直立，镜头大致水平。站稳片刻，让门框、窗框、墙角、标牌或固定家具进入画面。Hold the phone upright and roughly level. Stand still and include fixed features such as doorframes, window frames, corners, signs or furniture.
失败再补拍，不用原地连拍Retry after failure rather than taking a burst
先在同一站位换一个方向，或走一步后重拍。每张照片单独定位，结果对应最后一张成功照片的拍摄位置。Try a different angle from the same spot or move slightly before retrying. Each photo is localized independently; the result belongs to the latest successful photo.
尽量避开Avoid where possible
整面白墙、近距离物品、镜子反光、逆光窗户、模糊画面，以及被人群遮住的环境。Avoid blank walls, close-up objects, mirror reflections, backlit windows, blur and crowds blocking the scene.
取景建议用于提高成功率，不代表这些物体本身就是定位标记。程序匹配的是建图时已观察到的视觉特征。These framing suggestions improve feature coverage; the objects are not special localization markers. The program matches visual features observed during mapping.
