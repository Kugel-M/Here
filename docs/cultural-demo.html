Discover a place › Tap for a story › Connect with local culture › Keep a memory

A small “Tap for a story” marker invites a visitor to discover the story behind a Hong Kong dai pai dong. Reading, conversation and a meal turn an ordinary stop into a more personal encounter with the city.

The proposed cultural experience connects NFC story markers with place-based stories and a visitor's own record of the visit, complementing HERE's indoor wayfinding prototype.
